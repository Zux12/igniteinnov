Appointment Framework

Successful applicants may be offered an independent Business Development Associate appointment, subject to Ignite's selection and approval process.

12-Month Appointment

Appointment is for a defined 12-month period. Renewal is subject to Ignite's approval.

Independent Contractor

The opportunity is structured as an independent contractor engagement, not a salaried employment position.

Commission-Based Earnings

No fixed salary or guaranteed income. Commissions are subject to qualifying purchase orders and agreed payment terms.

Professional Representation

Appointed associates may receive official Ignite identification and authorization to introduce designated services.
