QUALITY & INTEGRITY

Contribution Standards

Certificates are issued only after the relevant contribution has been reviewed and accepted. Contributors must not submit plagiarised, fabricated, misleading, or improperly sourced work. AI-assisted work should be disclosed where appropriate.

Sensitive, confidential, personal, or third-party information must only be used where legally permitted and appropriately anonymised or protected.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there payment?

The contributor programme is generally voluntary unless otherwise agreed for a specific engagement or project.

Can students use the certificate for their CV?

Yes. The certificate records the contributor's role, project, contribution category, and contribution period.

Can I contribute to multiple projects?

Yes. Separate certificates may be issued for distinct accepted contributions.

Can teams participate?

Yes. Individual certificates may be issued to team members where their respective contributions can be identified.

How are certificates verified?

Third parties may contact IgniteInno Ventures with the Certificate ID and certificate copy for verification.
