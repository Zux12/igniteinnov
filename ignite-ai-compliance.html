Current Governance Position

Ignite AI™ is developed with a commitment to responsible AI governance, human-centred innovation, and Islamic ethical values. Guided by the principles of fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, and trust, we strive to build intelligent solutions that create meaningful impact for academia, industry, government, and society.

Clear and responsible claim

Ignite AI™ is guided by responsible AI governance and Islamic ethical values, combining advanced technology with human oversight to deliver fair, transparent, and accountable decision support across research, education, and innovation.

Our Seven Principles

These principles guide the design, deployment and continuous improvement of Ignite AI™.

🛡

Amanah — Trust

AI systems and submitted information must be handled responsibly, securely and only for their intended purpose.

⚖️

‘Adl — Justice

Assessments should use consistent criteria and must not unfairly disadvantage individuals or institutions.

🔍

Tabayyun — Verification

Ignite AI™ evaluates the information provided and identifies uncertainty rather than presenting assumptions as facts.

👤

Human Accountability

Important conclusions and final decisions remain under the authority of qualified human reviewers or committees.

🔒

Privacy and Dignity

Data minimisation, controlled access and confidential handling protect users and unpublished work.

🌱

Maslahah — Benefit

AI should deliver meaningful benefit to academia, industry and society while avoiding unnecessary harm.

📖

Transparency

Users should understand when AI is involved, what criteria are used and why a recommendation was produced.

Accuracy, Research Ethics and Confidentiality

Ignite AI™ is instructed to assess only the content supplied and to identify missing information rather than invent it.

🧠

Hallucination Controls

The system must not invent methodology, results, ethical approval, references or evidence that are absent from the submission.

🧪

Research Ethics Flags

Potentially missing consent, human or animal ethics approval, tissue-handling information and conflicts of interest may be flagged for human verification.

🔐

Confidentiality

Unpublished research and personal information should be accessed only by authorised parties and retained only as required for the stated purpose.

What Ignite AI™ Will Not Do

Responsible boundaries are as important as technical capability.

  • Issue fatwas or religious rulings
  • Claim to be a Shariah authority
  • Replace qualified scientific reviewers
  • Make unsupported professional claims
  • Use protected traits to lower scores
  • Present uncertain information as fact
  • Disclose confidential submissions
  • Make final high-impact decisions alone

Governance Roadmap

Our framework is intended to mature through documented controls, independent review and continuous improvement.

Phase 1

Internal Responsible AI Framework

Document human oversight, privacy, transparency, assessment boundaries and Islamic ethical principles.

Phase 2

Independent Ethical and Technical Review

Obtain external input on system behaviour, fairness, security, data governance and assessment controls.

Phase 3

Shariah Advisory Review

Invite a qualified adviser or panel to review the documented framework, terminology and intended use cases.

Phase 4

External Compliance Assessment

Assess Ignite AI™ against applicable Malaysian, international and Islamic AI governance frameworks when appropriate.

Ongoing

Continuous Improvement

Review outcomes, complaints, model changes, bias indicators and governance controls throughout the system lifecycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ignite AI™ formally Shariah-certified?

No. Ignite AI™ currently follows an internal responsible and Islamic ethical AI framework. Formal external Shariah review and compliance assessment are included in the governance roadmap.

Can Ignite AI™ replace a human reviewer?

No. It provides structured decision support. The appointed reviewer, committee or organisation remains responsible for the final decision.

Does Ignite AI™ issue fatwas?

No. Ignite AI™ does not act as a mufti or recognised religious authority and does not issue binding Islamic rulings.

Can an AI assessment be reconsidered?

Where the implementing organisation provides an appeal or reconsideration process, a qualified human should review concerns about extraction errors, rubric inconsistency or misunderstood content.

How does Ignite AI™ protect fairness?

The system is designed to apply predefined criteria consistently and, where practical, avoid unnecessary identifiers such as religion, nationality, gender, personal names and institutional prestige.

How is confidential research handled?

Ignite AI™ follows data-minimisation and restricted-access principles. The exact storage, retention and access arrangements depend on the implementation agreed with each organisation.
