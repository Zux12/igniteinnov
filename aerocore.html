About AeroCore

AeroCore is being developed as a next-generation Smart UVC platform that integrates ultraviolet disinfection with intelligent monitoring and automation. The platform is designed to support cleaner indoor environments while providing operational visibility through sensors, analytics and connected technologies.

The solution is intended for healthcare facilities, educational institutions, transportation hubs, offices, laboratories and other public spaces where maintaining environmental hygiene is essential.

Potential Applications

Healthcare

Hospitals, clinics, operating theatres, isolation rooms and outpatient facilities.

Education

Schools, universities, laboratories and research centres.

Public Facilities

Airports, offices, government buildings, transportation terminals and other indoor public environments.
